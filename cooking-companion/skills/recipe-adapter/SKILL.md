# Recipe adapter

Always run the adapted recipe back through **dietary-guard** before presenting it.

## Scaling

- Scale every ingredient, then use common sense. Eggs round to whole eggs (or use beaten egg by weight). Spices, salt and chili scale at about 75% when doubling, so season to taste. Leavening in baking needs care when scaling by more than double.
- Point out what *doesn't* scale linearly: pan size, cooking time, oven crowding, and reduction times.

## Substitutions

For each swap, give **what to use, how much, and what will change** (texture, flavour, cooking time).

- **Diet and allergy.** Choose substitutes that are themselves free of the user's other restrictions. For example, don't swap dairy for a nut milk when someone has a nut allergy.
- **Availability.** Use what's in `pantry.md` first: "No buttermilk? You have milk and lemons, so use 240 ml milk plus 1 tbsp lemon juice."
- **Baking is chemistry.** Be honest when a swap is risky (for example gluten-free flour in a yeast bread, or egg replacers in a soufflé). Suggest a recipe designed for the restriction instead.
- If you're unsure a swap works, say so, or check a technique source in `sources.md`.

## Units and regions

- Convert to the user's unit preference. For baking, prefer weights.
- Translate regional ingredient names (cilantro/coriander, heavy cream/double cream, all-purpose/plain flour) to the user's region.
- Convert oven temperatures, and note fan or convection ovens (usually about 20 °C / 25 °F lower).

## Equipment swaps

Adapt to what's in `kitchen-profile.md`: oven to air fryer, stovetop to pressure cooker or slow cooker, no stand mixer. Adjust times and temperatures, and give a doneness cue to check rather than relying on the clock alone.

## Skill, time and budget

- **Beginner:** fewer steps, explain each technique in one line, and suggest shortcuts such as pre-chopped veg or store-bought stock.
- **Short on time:** suggest parallel steps, quicker cuts and pan swaps, and give the realistic new total time.
- **Budget:** suggest cheaper proteins, seasonal produce, and ways to use the whole ingredient.

## Output

Present the adapted recipe cleanly, with changed lines marked ✏️ and a two-line summary of what changed. Offer to save it as a variant in `recipe-box.md`.
