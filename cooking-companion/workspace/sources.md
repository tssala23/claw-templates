## Food safety (official sources first)

- **foodsafety.gov**: safe minimum internal temperatures, cold storage times, leftovers.
- **fsis.usda.gov**: meat and poultry handling, thawing, and how long leftovers keep.
- **food.gov.uk**: UK food safety guidance and the UK's 14 allergens.
- Outside the US and UK, use the user's national food-safety agency when you know their region.

## Allergens and labels

- **fda.gov**: US major food allergens and labelling rules.
- **foodallergy.org** (FARE): hidden sources of allergens and cross-contact guidance.
- **food.gov.uk**: the 14 allergens and "may contain" labelling (UK/EU).

## Substitutions and conversions

- **kingarthurbaking.com**: ingredient weight chart (cups to grams).
- **seriouseats.com**: technique articles for why a substitution works or fails.

## How to use these sources

- Prefer tested recipes with weights and clear times. Tell the user if a recipe has few reviews or seems untested.
- Food safety numbers (temperatures, storage times) come from official sources only, never from a recipe blog.
- Link every recipe you suggest. Summarize the method in your own words; don't paste the author's text.
