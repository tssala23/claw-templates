# Sources: where to look

Gaming Companion checks here **before** a general web search for builds, meta, deals, completion and patches. Sites change: if a link is dead or stale, say so, fall back to search, and update this file. The player can add their own favourites in the last section.

## How to use these sources

- **Patch notes come from the official source first**: the developer's site, blog or official social account. Use stat sites for meta, not for what changed.
- **Check the page's "updated" date and patch or season label** before trusting a build. If it's older than the current patch, say so.
- **Cross-check meta claims.** If two sources disagree about the top pick, give both and say they disagree.
- **Stat sites (win rate, pick rate) and curated guides (expert picks) answer different questions.** Say which kind you used.
- **Many of these sites are JavaScript-heavy.** If a page won't load as text, try the site's article or guide pages, or search `site:<domain> <game> <topic>`.
- **Loadout and build codes**: some sites (such as CODMunity) publish in-game import codes. Pass them on exactly as written, with the date they were published.

## Multi-game build and meta sites

- **mobalytics.gg**: builds, tier lists and guides for many games.
- **maxroll.gg**: ARPG specialists (Diablo 4, PoE 2 and others).
- **u.gg / op.gg / metabot.gg**: win and pick-rate stats for competitive games.
- **tracker.gg**: personal stats for many shooters and competitive games.

## Deals and prices (weekly-digest)

- **gg.deals**: PC and console, with price history; it flags unofficial key resellers.
- **isthereanydeal.com**: PC; price history, bundles, giveaways.
- **steamdb.info**: Steam price history and sale dates.
- **psprices.com**: PlayStation, Xbox and Switch prices by region.
- **dekudeals.com**: Nintendo eShop and physical copies.

Only quote official stores or authorized resellers unless the player says grey-market keys are fine.

## Completion, length and achievements

- **howlongtobeat.com**: main story, main + extras, and completionist times.
- **trueachievements.com**: Xbox achievement guides and roadmaps.
- **psnprofiles.com**: PlayStation trophy guides and roadmaps.
- **steamhunters.app**: Steam achievement rarity and estimated times.
- **gamefaqs.com**: classic walkthroughs; great for older games.
