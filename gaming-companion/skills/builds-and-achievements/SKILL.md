# Builds, loadouts, patches and achievements

## Builds and loadouts

1. Find out the game, platform and version, and their current level or progress. Ask how they like to play (melee or ranged, safe or glass-cannon, solo or co-op) if it isn't already in their notes or profile.
2. **Check what's current.** Builds and meta depend on the patch. Open the game's row in `sources.md` first (meta sites for builds, official channels for patch notes), and use a general search only if the game isn't listed. Look up the current version and recent balance changes before recommending anything, and name the patch you're basing it on. If you can't verify it, say the advice may be out of date.
3. Recommend **one main build and one alternative**. For each, give the core idea in a sentence, the key stats, gear and skills, and what to prioritize *next* from where they are now. Don't recommend gear from areas they haven't reached without a spoiler check first ("The best weapon for this is in a later area. Want to know where?").
4. Save a chosen build to the game's `sessions/<game-slug>.md` under a `## Build` heading, so recaps and patch checks can refer to it.

## Patch impact

When they ask "did the patch change anything for me?", or when the weekly digest finds new patch notes for a game they're playing:

- Compare the patch notes with their saved build and the way they play.
- Lead with what affects *them*, such as "your main skill got a 10% cooldown increase". Then give one line of general changes. Link to the official notes.
- Don't call a build "dead" or "meta" from one patch-notes reading. Say how sure you are.

## Achievements and completion

1. Work from their progress notes and the achievement list. Look the list up using the completion sites in `sources.md` (TrueAchievements, PSNProfiles, Steam Hunters), and label achievements that are hidden or story-related **without naming** them unless the player opts into spoilers.
2. Order the route like this: **missables and points of no return first**, then anything tied to the current area, then grindy items, then multi-playthrough or difficulty achievements.
3. Mark online, co-op or time-limited achievements (event or server-dependent) clearly, and check whether they can still be earned.
4. Keep a checklist in `sessions/<game-slug>.md` under `## Completion` and tick items off as they report them.
5. Give a rough time estimate only when there's a source for it (HowLongToBeat or a completion guide), and say where it came from.

## Boundaries

Don't recommend save editing, unlock tools or exploits that break online rules or platform terms for achievements. Mention legitimate single-player glitches only if they ask.
