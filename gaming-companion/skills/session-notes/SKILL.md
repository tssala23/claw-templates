# Session notes and "where was I?"

## Files

There is one file per game: `sessions/<game-slug>.md`, for example `sessions/elden-ring.md`. Create it the first time you need it. Also update the game's `Last played` column in `games.md`.

## Logging a stop

When the player says they're stopping, or describes where they are, add a dated entry at the top of the game's file:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD
- Where: <area, chapter, mission or save slot>
- Doing: <current objective in the player's words>
- Next: <what they said they wanted to do next>
- Build/party: <only if mentioned>
- Notes: <loose ends, items to come back for, NPCs to revisit>
```

Record only what they told you. Don't fill gaps from wikis, because that's how spoilers creep in. Keep the file short: once there are more than about 10 entries, fold the oldest into a two-line "Earlier" summary.

## The "where was I?" recap

When they come back to a game:

1. Read their own notes first. Lead with **where they are, what they were doing, and what they planned next**, in three or four lines.
2. Add a quick **controls and mechanics refresher** only if they've been away more than about three weeks, or if they ask for it. Look up the game's controls if needed, and keep the refresher generic, with no story content.
3. Don't recap story beyond what they wrote. If they ask "what's happened in the story so far?", recap only up to their logged progress, and ask before going further.
4. If a patch has come out since their last session (check if you can), mention in one line that something changed, and offer details through builds-and-achievements.

## No notes yet

If there are no notes, say so and ask them where they think they are. Offer a spoiler-free check, like "Have you reached the second hub town yet?", to find their spot.
